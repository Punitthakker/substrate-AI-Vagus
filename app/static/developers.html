What the SDK does

  

    
Gate any button
data-substrate-check on Buy, Place bet or Confirm. Reads attributes or the surrounding form. Re-fires the click only if the person proceeds.

    
Closes the loop itself
Cancel records "followed", Proceed anyway records "ignored". The genome learns with zero extra integration.

    
Pacts in one tap
"Hold me to it until 9am". If they come back early, Substrate quotes their own promise.

    
Safe by default
Publishable keys locked to your domains, hashed user ids, per-IP limits, fail-open, shadow DOM isolation, text only.

    
Chat widget
data-mode="check,widget" adds a floating heart: an honest advisor that remembers each user.

    
Events for your app
substrate:verdict, substrate:proceed, substrate:cancel, substrate:pact on window.
