# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS, one self-contained file, deployed on Netlify (site `substrate-ai`). Chosen because the site must load instantly, run animation natively (SVG, canvas) and ship with no build step.

## Users

Two audiences, equally weighted (confirmed 2026-10-30):
- Investors and strategic partners (VC partners, NVIDIA) judging whether Substrate is a category-defining company. They read on a laptop between meetings or on a phone after one.
- Buyers and integrators at banks, wealth platforms, brokers and prediction markets judging whether Substrate is real, safe and embeddable.

## Product Purpose

Substrate is an emotional-intelligence and honesty layer that sits beneath any AI model. Its core, the Vagus Engine, remembers each person (the genome), reads the moment from their words, tells the truth about money and other high-stakes decisions in a chosen voice, predicts whether they will act on it, and records advice, action and outcome in a tamper-evident ledger. Success: a visitor understands the mechanism in one viewport and opens the live engine.

## Positioning

Every other assistant is trained on human approval and drifts toward agreement. Substrate separates what is true (computed facts with an Honesty Floor no model or voice can lower) from how it is said (vagal tone, eight voices), and owns the record of honest advice and its consequences, which the model labs structurally cannot build. The genome is inheritable across generations with consent.

## Operating Context

The site sits beside a live product: the console at vagus-engine-production.up.railway.app (/, /developers, /app, /admin), an embeddable SDK, an OpenAI-compatible harness, WhatsApp, and an Arabic mode. Visitors often arrive from a deck or an introduction, then click through to the live engine.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Live today: six tissues (wealth, venture, founder, wellbeing, prediction markets, FX and gold trading), Safety Protocol, eight voices, pacts, check-ins, Legacy Vault letters, inheritance, advisor desk, analytics, audit report, embed SDK, MCP server, WhatsApp channel, voice input and read-aloud, Arabic, 126 automated evals at 100%.
Constraints: no fundraising terms, raise amount or investment ask on the site (removed by the user, 2026-10-30). No "pre-seed" wording. Text-only emotion inference; never claim voice or face emotion analysis. Never claim customers, design partners or revenue that do not exist.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Substrate (Substrate AI). Engine: the Vagus Engine. Line: "Vagal tone changes how truth is delivered. Never whether." Colour meaning established in the deck: red is the heart (EQ), blue is the brain (IQ), green is life and outcome (red cells, white cells, growth). Body, nervous system, DNA and blood are the brand's governing metaphor (the user asked for brain-heart connection, X-ray views, blood flow, neurons, DNA). Founder: Punit Thakker, Dubai, punit@fracxn.com, punitthakker.com. The user requires the design not to look AI-made.

## Evidence on Hand

Live engine and its numbers (126/126 evals, six tissues, 14 model integrations). Public research the deck cites: OpenAI withdrew a model update in April 2025 for sycophancy; 2026 research on sycophantic AI and dependence; the intergenerational wealth transfer estimate used in the deck ($84T through 2045, Cerulli). No customers, partners, testimonials or press yet: do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

- Prove, don't claim: every section points at something the live engine actually does.
- Truth first, warmth around it.
- The body is the system: anatomy is how Substrate is explained, never decoration.
- Confidential by default, but no fundraising content on the public site.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Arabic-reading GCC audience: layouts must tolerate right-to-left phrases. Motion must respect reduced-motion preferences.
